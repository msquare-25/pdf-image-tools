How to Make a PDF Smaller for Online Assignment or Exam Uploads

Online assignment, exam, project, and student portals often reject PDFs when the file is too large. This usually happens with scanned handwritten pages, image-heavy notes, diagrams, certificates, or project reports. The goal is not just to reduce file size — the final PDF must still keep handwriting, diagrams, roll number, name, dates, and signatures readable.

Why assignment or exam PDFs become too large

  • Phone photos are high resolution and increase PDF size.
  • Scanned pages contain large borders or table background.
  • Duplicate or blank pages are included by mistake.
  • Diagrams and images make the PDF heavier.
  • Multiple PDFs are merged without cleanup.
  • Old drafts or rough pages remain inside the final file.

Before compressing, clean the PDF first

Many students directly compress the PDF, but that is not always the best first step. If the file contains blank pages, duplicate scans, large margins, or unnecessary rough pages, remove those first. A cleaner PDF compresses better and remains easier to read.

Step-by-step: reduce PDF size for assignment or exam upload

Step 1: Remove unnecessary pages

  1. Open Delete pages.
  2. Remove blank scans, duplicate pages, rough pages, old versions, or unrelated screenshots.
  3. Download the cleaned PDF.

Step 2: Crop large margins or scanner borders

Use Crop PDF to remove table background, scanner borders, large white margins, and extra empty space around handwritten or printed pages.

Step 3: Rotate sideways pages before upload

If any scanned page is sideways or upside down, fix it with Rotate PDF. This does not just improve presentation; it also helps reviewers read your work without extra effort.

Step 4: Compress the final PDF

  1. Open Compress PDF.
  2. Upload your cleaned PDF.
  3. Download the smaller PDF.
  4. Open the compressed file and check every important page.

Step 5: If pages are still images, make one clean PDF first

If your assignment or exam pages are still saved as JPG/PNG images, open Image → PDF, upload all pages, arrange them in order, and download one PDF. Then compress that final PDF if needed.

What to check after compression

  • Your name is readable.
  • Roll number / student ID is clear.
  • Question numbers are visible.
  • Handwriting is not too blurry.
  • Diagrams, formulas, tables, and graphs remain clear.
  • Teacher remarks, signatures, or declaration pages are readable if included.

Simple filename tips

A clean filename can also help avoid upload issues on some portals. Keep the name simple, short, and professional.

  • assignment-final.pdf
  • exam-answer-sheet.pdf
  • project-report-compressed.pdf
  • student-submission.pdf

Avoid emojis, brackets, very long filenames, and special characters like #, %, &, +, /, or ?.

Final checklist before uploading

  • File size is below the portal limit.
  • No blank, duplicate, or rough pages.
  • No unnecessary scanner borders.
  • No sideways pages.
  • Name, roll number, question numbers, diagrams, and signatures are readable.
  • Filename is simple and does not contain special characters.
  • The final PDF opens correctly before submission.
