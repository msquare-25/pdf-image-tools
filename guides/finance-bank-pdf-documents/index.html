Finance and bank PDF workflow

  1. Check the official checklist from the bank, lender, finance team, or portal.
  2. Convert document photos into PDF if IDs, receipts, forms, or signed pages are images.
  3. Merge related files when the portal or reviewer needs one combined PDF.
  4. Reorder pages so application forms, IDs, income proof, statements, and supporting files follow a clear sequence.
  5. Delete unnecessary pages like blanks, duplicates, old statements, unsigned drafts, or instruction pages.
  6. Crop margins to make statements, forms, and IDs easier to read.
  7. Rotate sideways pages before sending or uploading.
  8. Compress the final PDF and check that financial details remain readable.

Final checklist before uploading finance PDFs

  • All required documents are included according to the checklist.
  • Names, dates, account details, amounts, balances, statement entries and signatures are readable.
  • Pages are in correct order.
  • No blank, duplicate, outdated, or unsigned draft pages.
  • No sideways pages.
  • File size is below the upload limit.
  • Filename is simple, for example: loan-application-documents.pdf.
