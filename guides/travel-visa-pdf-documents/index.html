Travel and visa PDF workflow

  1. Check the official document checklist from the embassy, portal, university, employer, or agency.
  2. Convert document photos into PDF if passport pages, IDs, certificates, or letters are saved as images.
  3. Merge related files when the portal or reviewer needs one combined PDF.
  4. Reorder pages according to the checklist.
  5. Delete unnecessary pages like blanks, duplicates, old versions, and irrelevant screenshots.
  6. Crop margins to make passport scans, IDs, and forms easier to read.
  7. Rotate sideways pages before uploading.
  8. Compress the final PDF and check that details remain readable.

Final checklist before uploading travel or visa PDFs

  • All required documents are included according to the checklist.
  • Passport details, names, dates, stamps, signatures and document numbers are readable.
  • Pages are in correct order.
  • No blank, duplicate, old, or unrelated pages.
  • No sideways pages.
  • File size is below the upload limit.
  • Filename is simple, for example: visa-documents-final.pdf.
