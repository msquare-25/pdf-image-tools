Business documents often need to be shared as clean PDF packages. Use these workflows to merge, reorder, remove extra pages, crop margins, rotate pages, and compress PDFs before sending or uploading.

Professional business PDF workflow

  1. Collect all required documents — invoices, receipts, forms, reports, contracts, proofs, or supporting files.
  2. Merge related files into one PDF when the receiver should review everything together.
  3. Reorder pages so the main document comes first and supporting documents follow.
  4. Delete unnecessary pages like duplicates, blanks, old versions, or internal-only notes.
  5. Crop margins to remove scanner borders and improve readability.
  6. Rotate sideways pages before sending.
  7. Compress the final PDF so it is easier to email or upload.
  8. Use a clear filename such as client-proposal-final.pdf or invoice-package-march.pdf.

Final checklist before sending business PDFs

  • All required documents are included.
  • Pages are in correct order.
  • No blank, duplicate, outdated, or internal-only pages.
  • No sideways pages.
  • Names, dates, amounts, invoice numbers, signatures, and document details are readable.
  • File size is suitable for email or upload.
  • Filename is clear and professional.
